The LRU Cache (The FAANG Favorite)An LRU Cache is essentially how your web browser manages its history, or how your phone manages open apps.It has a fixed maximum capacity (e.g., it can only hold 3 items).It needs to look up items instantly ($O(1)$).It needs to insert items instantly ($O(1)$).The Catch: When it gets full, it must delete the item you haven't looked at in the longest time to make room for the new one.
